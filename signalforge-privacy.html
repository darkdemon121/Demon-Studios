SIGNALFORGE · CHROME EXTENSION

Local by design.

Last updated October 8, 2026

What the extension does

The SignalForge Chrome extension converts Markdown that you enter into sanitized Email HTML, Web HTML, or plain text. It can also render batches of Markdown documents and run structural checks. Rendering runs locally in the extension. The extension does not access webpage contents or send your Markdown to Demon Studios or another service.

Subscription and billing data

A paid SignalForge subscription is required to render in the extension. Checkout is handled by Stripe. When you activate a plan, the extension stores your Stripe Checkout session ID in local extension storage and sends it to Demon Studios to verify the subscription. For each render, the extension sends the Checkout session ID and render count to enforce your monthly quota; it does not send your Markdown or rendered output. SignalForge stores the Stripe customer ID, subscription billing-period start, and aggregate render usage in its database. Demon Studios does not receive or store your full payment-card number.

Data stored on your device

The extension saves your Checkout session ID, a preview counter, and a short local history containing render time, output format, input character count, and quality score. It does not save the Markdown source, rendered output, or payment-card details. You can remove the local data by clearing extension storage or removing the extension.

Permissions and websites

The extension asks for optional access to demon-studios.com only when you choose checkout, activate, render, or manage a subscription. That access verifies SignalForge subscription status and enforces render quotas. It does not read tabs or browsing history, inject code into webpages, or send your Markdown or rendered output to the billing service. You can revoke site access in Chrome extension settings; paid rendering will then pause.

Copying and deletion

When you choose Copy, the selected output is placed on your clipboard. You can remove the extension in Chrome to delete its locally stored Checkout session ID and render metadata. To cancel a subscription, use Manage subscription in the extension or the Stripe customer portal.

Local rendering and AI

Markdown is rendered locally in the extension after the subscription check. The extension’s paid rendering flow does not send your Markdown or output to Demon Studios. If a future feature sends content to an online processor, it will be separately disclosed before use.
