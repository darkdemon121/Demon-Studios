SIGNALFORGE · CHROME EXTENSION

Local by design.

Last updated October 3, 2026

What the extension does

The SignalForge Chrome extension converts Markdown that you enter into sanitized Email HTML, Web HTML, or plain text. It can also render batches of Markdown documents and run structural checks. Rendering runs locally in the extension. The extension does not access webpage contents or send your Markdown to Demon Studios or another service.

Data stored on your device

The extension saves a local preview counter and a short history containing render time, output format, input character count, and quality score. It does not save the Markdown source, rendered output, API keys, or payment details. This information stays in your browser’s extension storage on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Permissions and websites

The Chrome Web Store package requests no host permissions and no access to your tabs or browsing history. It does not inject code into webpages. It does not make network requests as part of the extension’s local rendering, quality checks, or batch preview.

Copying and deletion

When you choose Copy, the selected output is placed on your clipboard. You can clear the local render history in the Developer screen or remove the extension in Chrome to delete its locally stored data.

Payments and future services

The Chrome extension currently has no sign-in, account, subscription, or payment functionality. SignalForge’s planned paid API services are separate and are not activated by this extension release. Any future release that sends data to an online service will update this policy and clearly disclose what is sent and why.
