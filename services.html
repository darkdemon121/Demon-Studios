03PROJECT DETAILS

CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Good work starts
with a clear scope.

What should I bring to the first conversation?

A rough goal, who the product is for, any must-have features, your budget range, and preferred timing. Existing copy, imagery, sketches, or examples help, but are not required.

When is a timeline confirmed?

Timing is agreed after scope and milestones are clear. Website work starts once the required copy, images, and access are ready; app and extension timing depends on the approved feature set.

What is included in the CA$300 website option?

One responsive page using an existing layout, client-supplied copy and images, contact links, basic metadata, one revision round, and Vercel launch. A domain, paid services, and extra work are separate.

How are changes handled?

The written scope lists deliverables and review rounds. Work outside that scope is estimated and approved before it is added.

Who owns the accounts and project files?

Domain, hosting, and store accounts should be set up in your name where possible. Ownership, licenses, and handoff of project files are recorded in the project agreement.
